Welcome to Mount Roskill Grammar School Workshop Schedule

This website is designed to make it easier for students to discover, learn and
keep track with the upcoming available workshops at Mount Rosill Grammar School.

view upcoming workshops, find important information and stay up to date with the latest
opportunites availble for them!
